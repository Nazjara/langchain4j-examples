# Travel insurance tiers

| Tier | Medical limit | Trip cancellation | Baggage | Adventure sports | Excess |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BASIC | 50,000 EUR | not covered | not covered | not covered | 150 EUR |
| STANDARD | 250,000 EUR | up to 2,000 EUR | up to 1,000 EUR | not covered | 75 EUR |
| PREMIUM | 1,000,000 EUR | up to 10,000 EUR | up to 3,000 EUR | skiing, diving, hiking above 3,000 m | 0 EUR |

Travellers aged 65 or older pay 80% more on every tier.
