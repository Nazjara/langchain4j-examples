# Spring Brew Café — Guest Information

## Location
Spring Brew Café is at 12 Rynok Square, Lviv, Ukraine, on the ground floor of a 17th-century townhouse.

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday: 08:00–20:00
- Saturday: 09:00–22:00
- Sunday: 10:00–18:00
The café is closed on 1 January and 25 December.

## Menu highlights
- Signature drink: the "Bean Factory" flat white, made with oat milk by default.
- Breakfast is served until 12:00 every day.
- All pastries are baked on site each morning; the cinnamon roll is the best seller.
- Vegan options: every soup and two of the three sandwiches are vegan.

## Prices
- Espresso: 55 UAH
- Flat white: 85 UAH
- Cinnamon roll: 70 UAH

## Policies
- Free Wi-Fi; the password is printed on every receipt.
- Dogs are welcome on the terrace, not inside.
- Payment by card or cash; no cryptocurrency.
- Table reservations are accepted for groups of six or more, by phone only: +380 32 000 1234.
